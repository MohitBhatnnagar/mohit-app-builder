## Builder and judge friction fixed during the run

- Tools silently dropped unknown arguments (`pages` for `paths`, earlier `slug`, `search`): every tool now rejects
  them and lists the valid ones.
- `app_logs` gained `search`; `sendEmail` gained `attachments`; `reset_data` says to start the app again;
  `typecheck` says it covers src/ and server/; verify_app says how to get its screenshots.
- Guide: wrap seeds in `transaction()` so a crash leaves nothing behind; `full_page` screenshots; editing a
  migration is fine after `reset_data` while building; recharts' `fill` key trap; alias clashing join columns;
  SQLite upsert vs CHECK.
- **Port clash** (lettings builder): a stopped app's port was taken by another app, start_app said "running" and
  every check then tested the other app. Ports are now checked on all interfaces, the started app must answer with
  its own instance id, and start_app moves to a new port if the old one is taken.
- Data check: no more "100% NULL" for 99.96%; signed ledgers (`qty_change`, `stock_moves`) don't warn as negative.
- Screenshots flag paths that land on a not-found page; verify_app takes up to 20 paths.
